Part 1: Addition

Addition means putting numbers together.

The symbol for addition is: +

Example:

3 + 2 = 5

Think about having 3 apples and getting 2 more:

🍎🍎🍎 + 🍎🍎 = 🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎

So the answer is 5.

Important words:

In 3 + 2 = 5:

  • 3 → addend
  • 2 → addend
  • 5 → sum

The result of addition is called the sum.

Another example: 17 + 25

Break it down:

  17
+ 25
----
  42
  1. First add the ones: 7 + 5 = 12. Write 2 in the ones position and carry 1.
  2. Then: 1 + 2 + 1 = 4

Therefore: 17 + 25 = 42

💡 Pro Tip: Order does not matter in addition!
3 + 2 is the exact same as 2 + 3.
