Easy 🟢

  • 7 + 5 = 12
  • 15 − 8 = 7
  • 9 + 6 = 15
  • 20 − 13 = 7

Medium 🟡

  • 27 + 38 = 65
  • 64 − 29 = 35
  • -3 + 7 = 4 (Start at -3, move 7 units right on the number line)
  • 5 − 9 = -4 (Start at 5, move 9 units left past zero)

Absolute Value 🟣

  • |-7| = 7 (-7 is 7 units away from zero)
  • |12| = 12 (12 is 12 units away from zero)
