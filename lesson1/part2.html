Subtraction

Subtraction means taking one number away from another. It is one of the four basic arithmetic operations, used to find the difference between two quantities or to decrease a value by a given amount.

The symbol used to represent subtraction is called the minus sign:

−

Example:

8 − 3 = 5

Imagine you have 8 apples in a basket:

🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎

If you give away 3 apples to a friend, you take them away from your total:

🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎 🍎

Take away 3 → 5 apples remain.

So, written as an equation:

8 − 3 = 5

Important Math Vocabulary:

Each number in a subtraction problem has a specific mathematical name:

In the equation 8 − 3 = 5:

  • 8 → Minuend (the starting number that gets reduced)
  • 3 → Subtrahend (the number being subtracted or taken away)
  • 5 → Difference (the result left over after subtraction)

The final result of subtraction is called the difference. The expression itself (such as 8 − 3) is also referred to as the difference between those two numbers.

⚠️ Important Note: Order matters in subtraction!
8 - 3 is NOT the same as 3 - 8.
