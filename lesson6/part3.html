What is an Expression?

Part 3: Simplifying Addition

When you see multiple terms added together, follow these steps:

  1. Find all terms with the same letter.
  2. Add their numbers together.

Example 1:

Simplify: 4a + 3a + 2a

  • All terms have the letter a.
  • Add the numbers: 4 + 3 + 2 = 9
  • Answer: 9a

Example 2:

Simplify: 5x + 7 + 2x + 3

  • Group the x terms: 5x + 2x = 7x
  • Group the plain numbers: 7 + 3 = 10
  • Answer: 7x + 10
