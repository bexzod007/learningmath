What is an Expression?

Part 5: Simplifying Expressions with Multiple Terms

Sometimes you will see addition, subtraction, and different variables mixed together in one big expression. Don't panic! Just organize them by family.

Step-by-Step Guide:

Simplify: 9x + 4y - 3x + 2y - 5

  1. Find the x family: 9x - 3x = 6x
  2. Find the y family: 4y + 2y = 6y
  3. Find the plain numbers: -5

Combine everything together for the final simplified answer:

Answer: 6x + 6y - 5
