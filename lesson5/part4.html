Writing Expressions

Algebraic writing is simply converting word problems from real life into mathematical expressions. Think of yourself as a language translator!

1. Key Word Translation Dictionary

Operation Trigger Words English Phrase Math Expression
Addition (+) Sum, Plus, Increased by, More than, Total, Added to "A number increased by 7" n + 7
Subtraction (-) Difference, Minus, Decreased by, Less than, Subtracted from "10 decreased by a number" 10 - n
Multiplication (×) Product, Times, Multiply by, Twice (2×), Triple (3×), Of "Twice a number" 2n
Division (÷) Quotient, Divided by, Ratio of, Split into, Half of (÷2) "The quotient of a number and 4" n / 4

2. The Dangerous "Turnaround Words"

In addition and multiplication, order doesn't matter (3 + 5 is the same as 5 + 3). But in subtraction, order is critical!

Pay close attention to phrases with "THAN" and "FROM". They force you to swap the order of terms!

  • "5 less than x" $\rightarrow$ Start with x, then subtract 5.
    Correct: x - 5 (WRONG: 5 - x)
  • "Subtract 8 from y" $\rightarrow$ Start with y, then subtract 8.
    Correct: y - 8 (WRONG: 8 - y)

3. Two-Step Translation Examples

  1. "3 times a number, plus 5"
    → Step 1 (Multiplication): 3n
    → Step 2 (Addition): 3n + 5
  2. "4 less than double a number"
    → Step 1 (Double a number): 2x
    → Step 2 (4 less than that): 2x - 4
  3. "The sum of a number and 6, multiplied by 2"
    → Notice we must sum first! Use parentheses!
    → Answer: 2(n + 6)
