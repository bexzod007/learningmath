Exercise A: Identifying Parts of an Expression

For the expression 8x² - 3x + 15:

  1. How many terms are in this expression?
  2. What is the coefficient of x?
  3. What is the constant term?
  4. What is the variable?

Exercise B: Translating Words to Math

Write an algebraic expression for each phrase:

  1. A number n increased by 12
  2. 7 less than a number y
  3. The product of 5 and a number k
  4. 4 more than double a number x

Exercise C: Evaluating Expressions

Evaluate each expression when a = 3 and b = 5:

  1. 2a + b
  2. 4b - 3a
  3. a² + 2b
  4. 5(b - a)

Exercise A:

  • Number of terms: 3 (8x², -3x, and 15)
  • Coefficient of x: -3
  • Constant term: 15
  • Variable: x

Exercise B:

  1. n + 12
  2. y - 7 (Remember: "less than" turns the order around!)
  3. 5k
  4. 2x + 4

Exercise C:

  1. 2(3) + 5 = 6 + 5 = 11
  2. 4(5) - 3(3) = 20 - 9 = 11
  3. (3)² + 2(5) = 9 + 10 = 19
  4. 5(5 - 3) = 5(2) = 10
