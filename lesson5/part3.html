Coefficients

When a number is glued directly to the front of a variable, that number is called a coefficient. It simply tells you how many copies of that variable you have.

1. Visualizing Coefficients

  • 4x means x + x + x + x. The coefficient is 4.
  • 3a means a + a + a. The coefficient is 3.
  • -5y means 5 negative copies of y. The coefficient is -5.

2. The Invisible Coefficient Rule (The #1 Beginner Trap)

What is the coefficient of x when you just see the letter by itself?

If you see x, it means 1x. The coefficient is 1!

If you see -x, it means -1x. The coefficient is -1!

Memory Trick: If you buy an apple, you don't say "I bought 1 apple." You just say "I bought an apple." The number 1 is automatically understood. Algebra works the exact same way!

3. Comparing Components of an Expression

Let's dissect the expression: 9x² - 4x + 7

  • Variable: x
  • Exponents: 2 (on the first term)
  • Coefficients: 9 (for x²) and -4 (for x)
  • Constant: +7 (stands alone without any variable)
