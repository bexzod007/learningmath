Comparing Fractions

If two fractions have the same denominator, compare their numerators.

Example 1

3/8 vs 5/8

The denominators are both 8.

Compare:

3 < 5

Therefore:

3/8 < 5/8

Example 2

7/10 vs 4/10

7 > 4

Therefore:

7/10 > 4/10

💡 Remember: When the denominators are the same, the fraction with the bigger numerator is bigger.
