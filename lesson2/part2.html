Multiplying a Number by Parentheses

In algebra, when a number or variable is placed directly adjacent to a parenthesis (e.g., $4(x + 5)$), the multiplication sign is omitted by convention. The term outside is called the multiplier or factor.

The "Rainbow" or "Arrow" Method:

To distribute correctly, draw arrows from the outer multiplier to every single term inside the parentheses. Never stop at the first term!

Step-by-step Breakdown for $4(2x + 5)$:

  1. Identify the outer factor: $4$
  2. Identify all terms inside: $+2x$ and $+5$
  3. Multiply the outer factor by the first term: $4 \cdot 2x = 8x$
  4. Multiply the outer factor by the second term: $4 \cdot 5 = 20$
  5. Combine the results: $8x + 20$
