Challenge 🔴

  • Explain why 5/5 is an improper fraction.
    Answer: The numerator is equal to the denominator: 5 = 5. Therefore, 5/5 is an improper fraction. 5/5 = 1.
  • Explain the difference between a proper fraction and an improper fraction.
    Answer: A proper fraction has a numerator smaller than the denominator, for example 3/5. An improper fraction has a numerator greater than or equal to the denominator, for example 7/5 or 5/5.
