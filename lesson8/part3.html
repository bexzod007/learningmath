What is the Distributive Property?

Lesson 3: Solving Subtraction Equations

To undo subtraction, use its opposite operation: addition.

Your goal is to isolate the variable (get it all by itself on one side of the equation).

Example Problem: Solve y - 4 = 9

Step 1: Find the number to move.
We have - 4 next to y.
Step 2: Add 4 to both sides.
y - 4 + 4 = 9 + 4
Step 3: Calculate the answer.
y = 13

Result: The value of y is 13.
