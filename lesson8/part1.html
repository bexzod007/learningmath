Lesson 1: What is an Equation?

An equation is a mathematical statement showing that two things are equal. It always has an equals sign (=).

Think of an equation like a balanced scale. Both sides must have the exact same value.

Example: x + 3 = 7

Key Parts of an Equation:

  • Variable (x): An unknown letter. It represents the mystery number we want to find.
  • Operation (+): The action (addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division).
  • Equals Sign (=): Shows that the left side equals the right side.

Goal: Solving an equation means finding the value of the variable that makes the statement true.
