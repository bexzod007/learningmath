Lesson 6: Checking Your Answer

You can always know if your answer is 100% correct by doing a quick check!

How to Check: Put your answer back into the original equation instead of the variable.

Example:

Original Equation: x + 5 = 12

Your calculated answer was: x = 7

Step 1: Replace x with 7.

7 + 5 = 12

Step 2: Solve the left side.

12 = 12

Conclusion: Both sides are equal! Your answer is correct. 🎉
