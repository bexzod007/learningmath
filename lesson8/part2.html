What is the Distributive Property?

Lesson 2: Solving Addition Equations

To solve an addition equation, use the inverse (opposite) operation. The opposite of addition is subtraction.

Rule: Whatever you do to one side, you MUST do to the other side to keep it balanced.

Example Problem: Solve x + 5 = 12

Step 1: Find the variable.
The variable is x. It has + 5 next to it.
Step 2: Subtract 5 from both sides.
x + 5 - 5 = 12 - 5
Step 3: Simplify.
x = 7

Result: The value of x is 7.
