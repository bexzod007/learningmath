What is the Distributive Property?

Lesson 5: Solving Division Equations

Division is often written as a fraction, like m / 4 = 2.

To undo division, use the opposite operation: multiplication.

Example Problem: Solve m / 4 = 2

Step 1: Identify the operation.
The variable m is divided by 4.
Step 2: Multiply both sides by 4.
(m / 4) × 4 = 2 × 4
Step 3: Calculate.
m = 8
