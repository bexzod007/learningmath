Lesson 4: Solving Multiplication Equations

In algebra, 3a means 3 × a. When a number and letter are attached, they are multiplied.

To undo multiplication, use the opposite operation: division.

Example Problem: Solve 3a = 15

Step 1: Identify the operation.
The letter a is multiplied by 3.
Step 2: Divide both sides by 3.
3a / 3 = 15 / 3
Step 3: Calculate.
a = 5

Result: The value of a is 5.
