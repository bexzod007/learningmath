Distributing with Positive Numbers

When the multiplier outside the parentheses is positive, the original mathematical signs ($+$ or $-$) of the terms inside the parentheses remain unchanged.

Case A: Positive Multiplier with Addition

Example: $6(3y + 4)$

  • $6 \cdot 3y = 18y$
  • $6 \cdot (+4) = +24$
  • Final Expression: $18y + 24$

Case B: Positive Multiplier with Subtraction

Example: $5(4a - 3)$

  • $5 \cdot 4a = 20a$
  • $5 \cdot (-3) = -15$
  • Final Expression: $20a - 15$

Case C: Distributing to Three Terms (Trinomials)

Example: $2(x^2 + 3x - 5)$

  • $2 \cdot x^2 = 2x^2$
  • $2 \cdot 3x = 6x$
  • $2 \cdot (-5) = -10$
  • Final Expression: $2x^2 + 6x - 10$
