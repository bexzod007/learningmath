Composite Numbers

A composite number is a whole number greater than 1 that has more than two factors.

Example

Let's look at the number 12.

The factors of 12 are:

1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

Because 12 has more than two factors, 12 is a composite number.

Prime vs Composite

7 → Prime → only 1 and 7

8 → Composite → 1, 2, 4, and 8
