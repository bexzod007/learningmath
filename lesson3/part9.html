Easy 🟢

  • 7 → Prime number
  • 12 → Composite number
  • Factors of 10 → 1, 2, 5, 10
  • First five multiples of 4 → 4, 8, 12, 16, 20

Medium 🟡

  • Prime factorization of 18 → 2 × 3 × 3
  • GCD of 12 and 20 → 4
  • LCM of 4 and 6 → 12
  • GCD of 18 and 24 → 6

Challenge 🔴

  • 5 + 3 × 2 = 11 (Multiplication comes before addition)
  • (5 + 3) × 2 = 16 (Solve the parentheses first)
  • 20 − 4 × 3 = 8 (4 × 3 = 12, then 20 − 12 = 8)
  • LCM of 8 and 12 → 24
