GCD — Greatest Common Divisor

GCD stands for Greatest Common Divisor.

It is the largest number that can divide two or more numbers exactly.

Example

Find the GCD of 12 and 18.

Factors of 12:

1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

Factors of 18:

1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18

Common factors:

1, 2, 3, 6

The greatest one is 6.

Therefore:

GCD(12, 18) = 6

💡 Remember: GCD = the biggest factor that two numbers have in common.
