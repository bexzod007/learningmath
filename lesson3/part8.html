Order of Operations — PEMDAS / BODMAS

When a mathematical expression has several operations, we need to know which operation to do first.

PEMDAS

  • P → Parentheses
  • E → Exponents
  • M → Multiplication
  • D → Division
  • A → Addition
  • S → Subtraction

BODMAS is another common name for the same basic order:

  • B → Brackets
  • O → Orders / Exponents
  • D → Division
  • M → Multiplication
  • A → Addition
  • S → Subtraction

Example

2 + 3 × 4

We do multiplication first:

3 × 4 = 12

Then addition:

2 + 12 = 14

Therefore:

2 + 3 × 4 = 14

💡 Important: Multiplication and division come before addition and subtraction.

Example with Parentheses

(2 + 3) × 4

First solve the parentheses:

2 + 3 = 5

Then multiply:

5 × 4 = 20

Therefore:

(2 + 3) × 4 = 20
