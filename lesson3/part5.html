Simplifying After Distribution

Expanding expressions using the distributive property is often just the first step in algebraic simplification. Once parentheses are removed, you must identify and combine like terms (terms with identical variable components and exponents).

Standard Algorithm for Multi-step Simplification:

  1. Distribute: Clear all parentheses by performing multiplication.
  2. Group: Rearrange terms so that like terms are adjacent.
  3. Combine: Add or subtract the numerical coefficients of like terms.

Detailed Example 1: Expression with External Terms

Simplify: $3(2x + 4) + 5x - 7$

  • Step 1 (Distribute): $(3 \cdot 2x) + (3 \cdot 4) + 5x - 7 \longrightarrow 6x + 12 + 5x - 7$
  • Step 2 (Group): $(6x + 5x) + (12 - 7)$
  • Step 3 (Combine): $11x + 5$

Detailed Example 2: Multiple Parentheses

Simplify: $-2(3y - 1) + 4(y + 3)$

  • Step 1 (Distribute both): $-6y + 2 + 4y + 12$
  • Step 2 (Group): $(-6y + 4y) + (2 + 12)$
  • Step 3 (Combine): $-2y + 14$
