Prime Numbers

A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 that has exactly two factors: 1 and itself.

Example

Let's look at the number 7.

The factors of 7 are:

1 × 7 = 7

So the only factors are 1 and 7.

Therefore, 7 is a prime number.
